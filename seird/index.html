Model Details & Equations

This is an Agent-Based Model (ABM) that simulates a SEIR epidemic. It visualizes individual agent interactions, which collectively approximate the dynamics described by the following system of Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs):

dS/dt = - (β * I * S) / N
dE/dt = (β * I * S) / N - σ * E
dI/dt = σ * E - (γ + μ) * I
dR/dt = γ * I
dD/dt = μ * I
S: Susceptible
E: Exposed
I: Infectious
R: Recovered
D: Deceased
N: Total Population (S+E+I+R)
β (beta): Effective contact rate
σ (sigma): Latent rate (1/incubation period)
γ (gamma): Recovery rate (1/infectious period)
μ (mu): Mortality rate
